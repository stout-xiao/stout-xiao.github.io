## About this place

A note to the day, every day.

This is **Daily Insight** — a quiet corner for daily thoughts, technical notes,
and the occasional reading fragment. Posts will be short and honest.

## What you'll find here

- Lessons from learning, and the small tools that helped
- Daily reflections
- Code snippets, book quotes, and life notes — when they come

## Writing convention

Posts live under `src/content/posts/`. A minimal frontmatter looks like:

```yaml
---
title: Your title
published: 2026-05-11
description: One-line summary, used on the list page and SEO meta
tags: [tag-one, tag-two]
lang: en
---
```

Chinese versions go in the project root (`src/content/posts/`); English versions
live under `src/content/posts/en/` with the same filename.
